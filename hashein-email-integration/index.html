BEFORE YOU APPLY

Start with what you have.

There's no one-size-fits-all qualification. What you need depends on the work you want to do.

Qualifications

We consider a range of backgrounds: S.4 and S.6 certificates for Ugandan applicants, vocational and professional certificates, diplomas, bachelor's and master's degrees, and practical experience for suitable roles.

What to have ready

  • National ID or other valid identification
  • Passport, if you already have one
  • Education or training records when required for the role
  • Your date of birth and basic personal details

No passport or visa yet?

You can still start here. Our team can advise eligible applicants on passport applications and visa processing. Final approvals are made by the relevant authorities.
