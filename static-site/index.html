WORKING ABROAD

What to know before you go.

Practical notes for anyone preparing to work overseas — visas, contracts, agents, and your rights.

Visas & work permits

Never travel on a tourist visa to take up work. Confirm your work permit and employer paperwork before departure.

Employment contracts

Read your contract carefully. Role, salary, hours, accommodation and return terms should all be in writing before you sign.

Agents & official fees

Deal only with licensed agents. Hashein's official application fee is UGX 5,000 — anyone asking for cash “facilitation” fees is not acting for us.

Your documents

Keep copies of your passport, contract and IDs. Never hand original documents to a recruiter or agent.

Health & safety

Know your site safety rules, wear required protective gear, and keep emergency contacts close at hand.

Staying connected

Share your employer and accommodation details with family back home, and check in regularly once you arrive.

Verify every offer

Confirm the employer and role directly before paying anything or handing over documents.

Keep documents safe

Passports, contracts and IDs stay with you — never surrender originals to agents.

Know your rights

A work permit, a signed contract and timely pay are your entitlement, not a favour.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Contact our team and we'll help you understand the process.

Can I apply if I don't have a passport yet?

Yes. You can make an initial enquiry without a passport. If a suitable opportunity is available, our team can explain the passport process and applicable requirements.

Do I need a university degree?

No. Opportunities may suit people with secondary certificates, vocational or professional certificates, diplomas, degrees, or relevant practical experience. Each role has its own requirements.

Can I apply if I am not from Uganda?

Yes. We consider enquiries from across Africa, particularly East Africa. Select your country of origin in the application form.

What documents will I need?

Requirements vary by role. Please do not enter national ID or passport numbers into the website form; our team will explain document requirements directly.

Is a job or visa guaranteed?

No. Selection, job availability, documentation and visa approvals depend on the employer, eligibility and relevant authorities.
